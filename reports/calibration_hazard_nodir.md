## D runs between two team runs (`default` labels, frames)

Basis of `bridge_d_frames`: a short D between different teams would be a handover worth counting as a change. In IDSSE no such short D exists, and D between different teams lasts as long as D within a team (a real stoppage), so bridging is off.
